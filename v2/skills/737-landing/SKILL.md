# Boeing 737 landing and stop

## Final approach and touchdown

- Use the briefed landing-continuation procedure, with verified landing flap, VREF-based target speed, gear safe, speedbrake armed, autobrake selection, minimums datum, and missed-approach plan. A single CMD channel tracking the ILS is not an autoland. Do not improvise a manual landing because the second channel was never established.
- For dual-channel autoland, continuously monitor both CMD lights, VOR/LOC and G/S, FLARE and ROLLOUT arming, deviations, IAS, descent rate, and height. If FLARE is not armed by 350 ft or a required channel drops, execute `737-go-around`; do not repair it by repeated CMD presses or changing to heading/V/S/LVL CHG near the runway. Do not claim an unavailable LAND 3 annunciation.
- At the applicable decision altitude/height, verify the required visual reference and that landing remains safe. Monitor the documented FLARE, thrust-retard, touchdown, and rollout sequence; verify actual thrust reduction and flight response rather than assuming an armed mode will do it. A briefed manual landing requires the documented disconnect, thrust, flare, and directional-control procedure, not a sustained arbitrary pitch ratio.
- Use focused reads through the flare, as close to 1–2 seconds apart as tool latency permits. Confirm touchdown using on-ground/gear-contact evidence and the flight path; low height, a phase label, or a landing checklist mark is not proof of touchdown.

## Rollout and stop

- After confirmed main-gear touchdown, verify speedbrake deployment and deceleration. Apply reverse thrust and braking using the documented rollout controls and limitations, maintain centerline, and monitor groundspeed and runway remaining. If autobrake does not produce the expected deceleration, use the documented manual braking procedure; an autobrake selection alone does not guarantee stopping.
- Reduce/stow reverse as prescribed, and retain directional control through taxi speed. Disconnect rollout automation and use ground steering only at the procedure's conditions. Follow runway-exit/taxi authority; do not cross another runway or invent clearance.
- Once safely clear, or stopped where the scenario requires, read and complete the applicable after-landing section: verify the prescribed flap, speedbrake, autobrake, start-switch, light, and transponder states rather than applying them while still landing. Mark only confirmed actions with `737-checklist`.
- Keep observing until on-ground state, destination/runway position, near-zero groundspeed, and the required stopped/brake state are confirmed. Check warnings and checklist progress, then report the actual outcome and any unresolved issues. Do not stop tool use at G/S capture or touchdown when the task includes stopping.
