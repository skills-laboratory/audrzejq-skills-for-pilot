# Boeing 737 landing and stopping

## Verify continuation

- Confirm actual VOR/LOC and G/S capture, safe IAS, gear down/three green, and the selected landing flap configuration. At the procedure's capture condition, set the missed-approach altitude and immediately recheck that G/S remains active; a higher selected altitude is not protection against an unintended climb if G/S is lost.
- Continue only with required systems available and any non-normal landing assessment completed. Unless the assigned procedure is stricter, be stable by 1,000 ft in instrument conditions or 500 ft in visual conditions: landing configuration, target speed, controlled descent, acceptable approach deviations, and applicable checklist checks verified. Report unavailable indications and seek permitted alternate evidence; reporting alone does not establish landing eligibility. Go around if stability or landing eligibility cannot be established.
- For an authorized dual-channel autoland, engage the second channel using the documented hold action after capture while still above approximately 1,500 ft AGL; a short CMD press may change channels rather than add one. Verify both CMD indications, active VOR/LOC and G/S, and armed FLARE/ROLLOUT. If FLARE is not armed by 350 ft or a channel drops, use `737-go-around`, not repeated CMD presses. Observe the procedure's height datum; terrain AGL is not automatically radio altitude. Do not claim an annunciation that the interface does not expose.

## Flare and rollout

- Monitor IAS, height, descent rate, pitch, both CMD states, FMA, and approach deviations together through final and flare; repeated height-only reads do not establish continuing speed or lateral stability. Keep low-final work focused on flying, not troubleshooting or checklist bookkeeping.
- With verified autoland, observe the actual FLARE/ROLLOUT transitions, thrust retard, and decreasing sink rate without overriding a functioning flare. For a planned manual landing, follow the aircraft's manual-landing procedure, use small bounded pitch corrections with immediate feedback, and avoid excessive flare or prolonged float. Do not make a late manual conversion to rescue failed autoland.
- Confirm actual ground contact before reverse thrust or rollout actions. Verify spoiler deployment, braking, reverser state, directional control, and decreasing groundspeed. Check thrust-lever and measured engine response after a reverse command; do not equate a lever percentage with N1. Follow the documented reverse reduction/stow schedule and verify the response.
- Keep effective braking until safe taxi speed or the assigned stop. Do not turn autobrake OFF until manual braking is established. Verify any brake command through brake indications and actual deceleration, not an unverified toggle alone. Use parking brake only once stopped and verify groundspeed remains zero.

## Finish with evidence

- Perform the applicable after-landing procedure at its stated conditions, preserving runway lights and configuration until the prescribed transition. Remain on the runway only if the scenario's stop objective requires it; otherwise use the assigned exit/taxi clearance.
- Before declaring completion, read actual on-ground state, groundspeed, position/runway evidence, checklist progress, and warnings/system annunciators. Reassess unresolved cautions using their non-normal procedure and report any remaining condition; stopping does not prove warnings are clear.
- Report observed touchdown and rollout separately from landing quality. Without measured touchdown sink/g and valid runway alignment evidence, do not claim a gentle touchdown or centerline landing from a phase label, centered ILS needles, or autoland engagement alone. Missing or inconsistent readings are limitations to report, not reasons to invent measurements or unsafe corrective controls.
