# Boeing 737 landing and rollout

## Final and flare

- Continue only with the stable approach, landing configuration, visual/authorization conditions and verified speed established in `737-ils-approach`. Keep monitoring modes and deviations; go around for lost guidance or unstable flight rather than changing to heading, level-change or vertical-speed modes close to the runway.
- For documented autoland, maintain both channels and verify FLARE activates and the thrust retard actually occurs. Do not send sustained joystick inputs or enable manual override while expecting the autopilot to flare. If the required channel/mode evidence is lost before touchdown, execute the documented go-around.
- For a planned manual landing, use the documented disconnect/override sequence while stable and monitor continuously. With visual runway alignment, begin a small, smooth flare around 20–30 ft over the runway and reduce thrust toward idle as prescribed. Use brief pitch corrections with immediate feedback, not a fixed held ratio; do not chase a zero descent rate, balloon or force a high/fast aircraft onto the runway. Height above surrounding terrain is not necessarily height above the runway.

## Touchdown and stop

1. Verify main-gear contact from available ground/contact indications and the aircraft response at the destination runway. Maintain centerline and gently lower the nose. A low AGL reading or radio callout is not touchdown evidence.
2. Verify thrust idle, spoiler deployment and autobrake deceleration. Select permitted reverse only after confirmed touchdown and verify its response. Track the runway using the documented rollout guidance or small directional inputs; use appropriate manual braking if automatic deceleration is absent.
3. Reduce and stow reverse at the speed stated by the procedure and continue braking to a verified stop. Do not assume ROLLOUT stops the aircraft. Confirm on-ground state, near-zero groundspeed, runway/destination position and warning status with successive reads.
4. Record each supported landing and after-landing checklist item with `737-checklist` once control permits. Report success only after landing and stopping; report any unresolved warning or configuration instead of claiming it is clear.
