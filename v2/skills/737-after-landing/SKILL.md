# Boeing 737 rollout and after landing

## Retain control through rollout

- Follow the documented landing continuation. Confirm touchdown and ground state, spoiler deployment, braking and reverser response; monitor centerline and ground speed. Do not begin cleanup while directional control or stopping needs attention.
- Reduce and stow reverse thrust at the prescribed speed and verify both reversers stowed. Take over and disengage rollout automation at the procedure's handoff, verifying engagement status; do not disconnect while still relying on it for directional control.
- Once safely clear of the runway or stopped as required by the scenario, execute the after-landing section using `737-checklist`. Verify autobrake OFF, speedbrake DOWN, flap retraction, required probe-heat and start-switch positions, radar, lights and transponder. Set the parking brake only after confirming the aircraft is stopped.

## Check warnings after the final configuration change

1. Read `fire-and-emergency` master caution and system annunciators, plus the available warning readings. A routine flight-instrument/FMA read does not include every warning. Repeat this scan after cleanup: probe-heat OFF can produce expected pitot/anti-ice indications and a new caution even if an earlier scan was clear.
2. For any illuminated system, read the relevant source indications and use its documented procedure. Distinguish an expected ground indication from a fault requiring correction; do not undo required ground configuration merely to extinguish a light.
3. Acknowledge an understood, resolved condition or an expected ground caution only as its procedure permits, then read the master caution and source indications again. Recurrence or an unexplained indication remains unresolved. Never silence a horn, inhibit GPWS or reset a caution as a substitute for diagnosis.
4. Verify stopped/on-ground state, idle thrust, stowed reversers, required ground configuration and actual warning status before finishing. Report unresolved or expected remaining indications accurately rather than claiming warnings clear from checklist completion or the arrived phase.
