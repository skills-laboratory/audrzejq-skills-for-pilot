# Boeing 737 descent and approach preparation

- Read actual altitude, IAS, vertical speed, configuration, FMA, and distance to the approach. Preserve functioning automation while preparing; an ALT HOLD label or selected altitude does not prove level flight at that target. After each CDU page/entry cycle, recheck flight instruments, FMA, and approach distance before another cycle; correct drift before secondary tasks. Estimate minutes to the intercept as 60 times remaining NM divided by groundspeed in kt. If preparation will not finish in that distance, use an authorized level-off, vector, or hold within chart/clearance constraints rather than continuing toward the runway unprepared.
- Read the selected approach chart, live descent/approach checklists, landing continuation, and missed approach before intercept. Identify runway, ILS frequency/identifier, magnetic course, distance source, segment altitudes, minima datum, and missed-approach track/altitude. Different DME stations and threshold distance are not interchangeable; obtain the chart's required reference rather than assuming the ILS DME matches it.
- Set local QNH with the correct pressure units and verify the required altimeters and pressure-reference modes. Set pressurization landing altitude from the destination elevation, not the decision height; perform the prescribed annunciator recall and resolve actual warnings.
- For BARO minimums, enter the published DA/MDA in feet AMSL, not the parenthesized height above threshold. If only a height is supplied, use its stated datum elevation to obtain the altitude and cross-check it against threshold elevation. Use RADIO minimums only when the procedure authorizes them; simulator height above terrain is not automatically height above the runway. Read back both selector mode and value.
- Complete and mark independent preparation items, including the prescribed altimeter, pressurization, minima, radio/course, sign/light, and autobrake checks, without making them wait for a missing VREF. Read both applicable descent and approach checklist sections during arrival preparation and mark each verified condition, including an already-correct state, using its live identifiers; do not repeat a correct switch action just to obtain a mark.
- Obtain landing weight and units from aircraft readings and the CDU instructions. Check the page's weight scale as well as its units: a decimal gross-weight field may require thousands of kg or thousands of lb, not raw kg. For a documented thousands-of-kg field, divide the kg reading by 1,000; for thousands of lb, convert kg to lb first, then divide by 1,000. Use the displayed precision and configured entry path, including any gross-weight entry inhibit; do not assume the unit system or improvise a ZFW entry.
- Read the page title and field labels before typing. After INVALID ENTRY, CLR can dismiss the message while restoring the rejected text; further CLR presses erase characters. Verify an empty scratchpad before typing the corrected value, then verify the accepted weight and selected landing flap/VREF field. Generated VREF choices or scratchpad text alone do not prove selection. Set the weight/configuration-dependent approach speed with its prescribed correction and verify MCP speed and actual IAS.
- If weight/VREF is still unavailable after checking the documented format and entry path, report the specific issue and maintain a safe flight path while seeking an approved performance source or repositioning. A navigation-database date warning and an entry-format rejection are different problems; reconcile the selected chart and receiver identification without changing navigation data in flight. Do not guess VREF, treat the initial arrival speed as a landing speed, or proceed to final landing configuration without valid performance data.
- Set required passenger signs, lights, navigation receivers/courses, and autobrake; verify identifiers and decoded selector detents. A raw numeric selector reading is not necessarily the printed panel setting: use the documented mapping before declaring a mismatch. Report unresolved inconsistencies without repeatedly cycling a functioning selector. Record each verified item with `737-checklist` while flight is stable.
- Choose the authorized intercept altitude from the chart and clearance, not an arbitrary round-number floor. Before leaving altitude hold, verify the descent mode, selected altitude, and selected rate; mode activation can synchronize targets, so read them again afterward. Recheck actual descent and IAS. If descent prevents deceleration, revise the rate or level safely within procedure limits and configure at the documented speeds; do not chase a lower altitude while fast and unconfigured. Continue with `737-approach`.
