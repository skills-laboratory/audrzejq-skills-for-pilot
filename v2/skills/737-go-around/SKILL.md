# Boeing 737 go-around and missed approach

- Brief the aircraft go-around procedure and selected chart's missed approach before intercept. Use them when capture fails, the approach is unstable at its gate, required guidance/autoland capability is lost, visual reference is inadequate at minimums, or landing is otherwise unsafe. Do not substitute an arbitrary level-off below chart constraints or a final progress message for executing the missed approach.
- Initiate the prescribed TO/GA action and verify actual go-around thrust and FMA response. Establish the procedure's pitch/speed guidance and initial flap selection; use automation only with confirmed modes and safe response. If it fails or commands the wrong path, disconnect as documented and control pitch, roll, and thrust manually with immediate instrument feedback.
- Confirm positive climb from altitude and vertical-speed indications before retracting gear. Follow the published missed-approach track, altitude, turn restrictions, and climb requirements; confirm MCP targets and actual flight path, not just switch positions. Retract flaps only on the prescribed acceleration and maneuver-speed schedule.
- Prioritize flight control until a safe climb or level segment is established, then record verified go-around actions and report the reason. Continue monitoring and flying the missed approach; prepare another approach only when safe and authorized. A go-around is not arrival or permission to leave the aircraft unattended.
