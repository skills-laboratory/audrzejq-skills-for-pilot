# Boeing 737 go-around

- Brief the selected chart's missed-approach track, altitude, and restrictions before final. Use the aircraft go-around procedure for thrust, modes, pitch guidance, flap selection, and acceleration; the chart supplies navigation, not aircraft configuration.
- Initiate promptly when required landing conditions fail. Select TO/GA using the documented control and immediately read both thrust levers, measured N1, A/T state, CMD states, and active go-around modes. With A/T disconnected, advance thrust manually to the procedure's go-around setting as part of initiation; do not wait for TO/GA to move the levers. If expected automatic thrust advancement is absent, apply the prescribed thrust manually before flap or other secondary calls, verifying engine response. A TO/GA pitch annunciation alone proves neither thrust nor autopilot control; with CMD disconnected it is flight-director guidance only.
- Follow valid go-around pitch guidance with functioning automation or verified direct control, reading IAS, pitch and its trend, bank, height, and vertical speed after each change. For manual pitch, ease toward neutral as the commanded attitude is reached, rather than leaving a reduced positive ratio held while vertical speed catches up. If the remaining attitude change will be consumed by tool latency, remove the input now and read again; neutral is not an attitude lock. Arrest an excessive pitch-up or falling IAS with the recovery guidance in `737-climb`, not sustained back-pressure for more climb. Do not infer a reversed control sign from one response or amplify unexplained inputs to calibrate the control on low final.
- Select the prescribed go-around flap setting, normally flaps 15 from flaps 30/40, and verify movement. Maintain safe maneuver speed and the procedure's initial climb target; do not retract all flaps or chase climb rate with sustained nose-up input.
- Verify positive climb from rising altitude and positive vertical speed before gear UP. Follow the missed-approach track and selected altitude restrictions; do not invent an early turn or follow an unverified FMC route.
- Before relying on reconnected CMD, stabilize the aircraft within engagement limits, neutralize sustained pitch/roll inputs, and release manual overrides through the documented handoff. Verify CMD, active modes, targets, and the actual path over successive reads; ALT HOLD does not command a climb to a newly selected altitude. A/P does not restore disconnected A/T: continue manual speed/thrust control unless an active thrust mode and its response are verified, and reduce go-around thrust as the procedure requires for level-off. If the path still diverges after a clean handoff, recover with verified controls and report the unresolved limitation; do not assume a mode label proves control.
- Use the stabilization and scheduled-cleanup guidance in `737-climb`, preserving the missed-approach track and go-around conditions rather than repeating departure checks; load `737-altitude-hold` before capture. Resolve the reason for the discontinued approach before another attempt, and mark go-around checks only from observed aircraft evidence.
