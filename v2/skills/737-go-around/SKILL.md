# Boeing 737 go-around

- Brief the selected chart's missed-approach track, altitude, and restrictions before final. Use the aircraft go-around procedure for thrust, modes, pitch guidance, flap selection, and acceleration; the chart supplies navigation, not aircraft configuration.
- If preparation fails while still level outside final with verified HDG SEL/ALT HOLD, use a permitted vector or hold at a safe assigned altitude and verify the new path before resuming setup. This is repositioning, not a landing-configured go-around: do not add TO/GA, flap changes, or a descent solely because an FMC entry failed. If a climb is required, use its documented mode and verify actual climb; if descending on final or otherwise unsafe, perform the go-around promptly.
- For an actual go-around, select TO/GA using the documented control, establish the procedure's go-around thrust, and verify pitch/roll guidance and the actual path before troubleshooting. Immediately read both thrust levers, measured N1, A/T state, CMD states, and active go-around modes. With A/T disconnected, advance thrust manually as part of initiation; do not wait for TO/GA to move the levers. If automatic thrust advancement is absent, apply the prescribed thrust manually before flap or other secondary calls, verifying engine response. A TO/GA pitch annunciation alone proves neither thrust nor autopilot control; with CMD disconnected it is flight-director guidance only.
- Selected altitude or positive V/S cannot pull the aircraft out of captured G/S, and selected heading cannot replace captured VOR/LOC. Use the documented TO/GA or approach-mode exit and verify new active modes. Toggling one flight director OFF/ON or reconnecting CMD does not prove captured modes were reset. Preserve functioning go-around automation; if it continues unsafe descent or impedes recovery, disconnect and establish verified manual thrust and attitude without more mode-button experiments.
- Follow valid go-around pitch guidance with functioning automation or verified direct control. In manual flight, choose the procedure's attitude appropriate to speed/configuration and control pitch rate, not lagging V/S. Compare successive actual pitch reads as well as the displayed trend, which can lag a reversal. Estimate time to the attitude from pitch rate, allowing for reading, decision, and control latency; ease to neutral before the next cycle would pass it. Use brief counter-corrections to arrest residual motion, but do not increase opposite input once pitch is moving toward the target merely because V/S remains large. Read pitch, IAS, bank, height, and V/S after each correction, as close to 1–2 seconds apart as latency permits. Neutral is not an attitude lock: require successive reads with arrested pitch motion and safe IAS before secondary calls. Use `737-climb` for excessive pitch-up or falling IAS; do not infer a reversed control sign from one response or amplify inputs to calibrate the control on low final.
- Once attitude and speed are stable, relieve persistent elevator force with small trim increments in the documented direction, verifying trim and pitch response after each. Do not use large absolute trim jumps to chase V/S or combine unexplained elevator, trim, and thrust changes; stabilize with direct controls first and recheck after thrust/configuration changes.
- Select the prescribed go-around flap setting, normally flaps 15 from flaps 30/40, and verify movement. Maintain safe maneuver speed and the procedure's initial climb target; do not retract all flaps or chase climb rate with sustained nose-up input.
- Verify positive climb from rising altitude and positive vertical speed before gear UP. Follow the missed-approach track and selected altitude restrictions; do not invent an early turn or follow an unverified FMC route.
- Before relying on reconnected CMD, stabilize within engagement limits and neutralize sustained pitch/roll inputs. Read `flight-controls.joystick-override`, release it using the documented control, and verify OFF before the handoff; zero pitch-control alone does not release an override. Verify CMD remains engaged, intended active modes/targets, and the actual path over successive reads; ALT HOLD does not command a climb to a newly selected altitude. A/P does not restore disconnected A/T: continue manual speed/thrust control unless an active thrust mode and its response are verified, and reduce go-around thrust as the procedure requires for level-off. If the path still diverges after a clean handoff, recover with verified controls and report the unresolved limitation; do not assume a mode label proves control.
- Use the stabilization and scheduled-cleanup guidance in `737-climb`, preserving the missed-approach track and go-around conditions rather than repeating departure checks; load `737-altitude-hold` before capture. Resolve the reason for the discontinued approach before another attempt, and mark go-around checks only from observed aircraft evidence.
