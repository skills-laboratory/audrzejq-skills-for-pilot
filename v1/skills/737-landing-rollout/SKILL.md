# Landing and rollout

1. Monitor airspeed, altitude, vertical speed, approach deviations, configuration and both autopilot channels. Apply documented stabilized-approach gates and go around if the required state is not maintained. Compare BARO minimums with QNH altitude, not terrain AGL; at minimums, require authorized visual reference and continuation conditions.
2. For autoland, verify FLARE and ROLLOUT armed and both channels engaged. If FLARE is not armed by 350 ft or a required channel drops, discontinue autoland and follow the go-around procedure. Do not repair late channel loss by repeatedly pressing CMD or changing approach modes near the runway.
3. Monitor flare and thrust reduction without overriding correctly functioning autoland. Verify actual touchdown/on-ground indications before reverse thrust; maintain centerline and verify speedbrake deployment and braking/deceleration.
4. Reduce reverse thrust around 60 kt and stow it as required by the aircraft procedure; verify both reversers and thrust state. Maintain directional control and brake to the required stopping or taxi condition. Set and verify parking brake only after stopping.
5. Disconnect automation at the documented rollout point. Verify both CMD channels and autothrottle state, then acknowledge disengagement alerts using documented reset controls and read warning indications. Do not reengage automation simply to silence an alert.
