# After landing

1. Confirm on-ground state, runway position and the assigned after-landing point before cleanup. Do not retract flaps or stow landing equipment during high-speed rollout. If the goal is to stop on the runway, verify zero groundspeed, idle thrust, stowed reversers and parking brake set; do not invent a taxi requirement.
2. Follow the after-landing checklist: autobrake OFF, speedbrake DOWN, probe heat OFF, exterior lights as required, engine-start switches OFF, weather radar OFF, flaps UP and transponder as required. Verify each actual indication, including completed flap travel, before marking its item. Keep engines and power sources in the state required by the goal rather than performing an unrequested shutdown.
3. After cleanup, read warning/alarm and fire-and-emergency indications, including master caution and system annunciators. Also verify autopilot/autothrottle disengagement and associated alerts. Flight/configuration readings alone are not a complete warning scan.
4. Inspect illuminated system indications and apply their documented procedures. Acknowledge expected disengagement or ground-configuration cautions only as permitted, then reread after reset to detect recurrence. Do not undo required probe-heat or other ground settings merely to extinguish indications, and never inhibit protection to obtain a clear panel. Report persistent expected or unresolved indications explicitly.
5. Reread checklist progress and reconcile every outstanding item with aircraft evidence or a supported applicability decision. Report any service that cannot represent a legitimate not-applicable condition; do not claim full completion or warnings clear without corresponding observations.
