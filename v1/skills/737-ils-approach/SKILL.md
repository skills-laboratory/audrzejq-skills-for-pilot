# ILS approach

1. Verify assigned altimeter settings, passenger signs and applicable landing lights. For "Update RNP — As needed", inspect the selected procedure's navigation-performance requirements and the applicable FMC performance page before deciding whether an update is needed. An empty route or an ILS selection alone does not establish that this check is unnecessary; report unavailable evidence rather than inventing it.
2. Establish planned landing flaps. Apply GPWS flap inhibit only for an authorized flaps-15 landing, not temporary flaps 15 during configuration. For normal flaps-30/40 landing, verify normal flap-warning protection and record why the conditional inhibit action does not apply, using supported checklist applicability handling.
3. Intercept on a suitable heading with glide slope above the aircraft and within charted restrictions. Arm APP once; read active and armed flight-mode annunciations. Distinguish APP arming, active VOR/LOC capture, glide slope alive and active G/S capture; the APP button alone does not prove capture. If the glide slope passes without capture, follow the documented missed approach rather than chasing it with a lower MCP altitude, V/S or repeated APP presses close to the runway.
4. Reduce speed and extend flaps progressively within published limits. At glide slope alive, select gear DOWN and verify nose, left and right safe indications; select flaps 15, both engine-start switches CONT and speedbrake ARM, verifying configuration and the armed indication.
5. At confirmed G/S capture with sustained descent, select landing flaps and verified VREF plus the applicable correction. Set charted missed-approach altitude at the documented condition, then reread active pitch mode, vertical speed and deviations to ensure descent on G/S continues. An unchanged G/S label does not prove correct tracking if the aircraft levels or climbs. Complete landing checks using observed gear, flap, speedbrake and flight-mode evidence without delaying the autoland setup.
6. If autoland is authorized, freshly verify both receivers still identify the same ILS, their courses, the required flight-director states and which CMD channel is engaged. After capture, while above approximately 1,500 ft AGL, hold the inactive channel once using the documented hold action and duration parameters, not a short CMD press. Verify both CMD channels remain engaged with active VOR/LOC and G/S, and FLARE and ROLLOUT arm as specified by the aircraft guide. Do not claim annunciations the tools do not expose.
7. If a hold merely swaps channels, dual-channel operation failed: stop alternating CMD buttons or extending guessed hold durations. Resolve receiver tuning and prerequisites only with sufficient altitude and time; otherwise discontinue autoland and follow the documented go-around procedure. After any channel change, immediately check pitch, vertical speed and deviations as well as both CMD states, then reread promptly for developing divergence. Do not retry channel engagement below the setup gate to salvage autoland.
